Privacy Policy for Luxa

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Luxa ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Luxa camera application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data that can be used to identify you. The App uses your device's camera to capture photos, which are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your photos.

3. In-App Purchases

Our App offers in-app purchases and subscriptions, processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Google. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: Required to take photos. We do not access your camera at any other time.
  • Write External Storage (or Media): Required to save the photos you capture to your device's storage.

5. Data Safety

We do not collect any user data. Your photos are stored locally and are not transmitted to us or any third party.

6. Hosting

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security purposes. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
